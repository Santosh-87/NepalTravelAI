# Nepal National Park & Protected Area Entry Fees

**Source:** https://ntb.gov.np/plan-your-trip/before-you-come/park-entry-fees
**Last updated:** February 27, 2026

---

## Overview

A park entry permit must be obtained to enter all protected areas in Nepal. Fees can be paid at:
- Tourist Service Center, Bhrikutimandap, Kathmandu
- Entry points of each park

Different fees apply based on nationality:
- **Nepalese citizens** — lowest rate
- **SAARC Nationals** (India, Pakistan, Bangladesh, Sri Lanka, Bhutan, Maldives, Afghanistan)
- **Foreign Nationals** (all others)
- **Children below 10 years** — free at all parks

---

## UNESCO World Heritage Site National Parks

### Chitwan National Park
- Entry fee for foreign nationals to visit Chitwan National Park: NPR 2,000 per person per entry
- Entry fee for SAARC nationals to visit Chitwan National Park: NPR 1,000 per person per entry
- Entry fee for Nepalese to visit Chitwan National Park: NPR 150 per person per entry
- Children below 10 years: Free
- Note: 13% VAT applies on top of entry fees

### Sagarmatha National Park (Everest Region)
- Entry fee for foreign nationals to visit Sagarmatha National Park: NPR 3,000 per person per entry
- Entry fee for SAARC nationals to visit Sagarmatha National Park: NPR 1,500 per person per entry
- Entry fee for Nepalese to visit Sagarmatha National Park: NPR 100 per person per entry
- Children below 10 years: Free
- Note: Required permit for Everest Base Camp Trek and Gokyo Lakes Trek
