# NTB Park Entry Fees

Source: https://ntb.gov.np/plan-your-trip/before-you-come/park-entry-fees
Last updated: February 24, 2026

## Overview

Park entry permit must be obtained to enter protected areas. Park entry fees may be paid at Tourist Service Center, Bhrikutimandap, Kathmandu, or at the entry points. Please refer to the table below for details about payment. Please note that the information below is as per the information provided by the concerned department. Any difference or discrepancy can be reported at infontb.org.np . Updates will be made after verification from official sources.
