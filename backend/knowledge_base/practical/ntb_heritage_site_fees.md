# Nepal Heritage Site Entry Fees

**Source:** https://ntb.gov.np/plan-your-trip/before-you-come/heritage-site-entry-fees
**Last updated:** February 27, 2026

---

## Overview

Entry fees must be paid at the entry point to access Heritage Sites and museums in Nepal. Different fees apply based on nationality:
- **Foreign Nationals** (non-SAARC, non-Chinese)
- **SAARC Nationals** (India, Pakistan, Bangladesh, Sri Lanka, Bhutan, Maldives, Afghanistan)
- **Chinese Nationals**
- **Nepalese citizens** (most sites free or heavily discounted)
- Children below 10 years are free at most sites.
