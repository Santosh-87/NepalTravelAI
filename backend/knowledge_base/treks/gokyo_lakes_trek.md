# Everest Gokyo Lakes Trek

**Source:** https://www.nepalhikingteam.com/package/everest-gokyo-lake-trek
**Last updated:** February 27, 2026

---

## Overview

The Everest Gokyo Lakes Trek is a 15-day journey through the Khumbu region to the sacred Gokyo Lakes — the world's highest freshwater lakes — and Gokyo Ri (5,360m), offering panoramic views of four 8,000-meter peaks.

- **Destination:** Nepal
- **Duration:** 15 Days
- **Difficulty:** Moderate
- **Max. Elevation:** 5,364m (Gokyo Ri)
- **Best Time:** March–May & September–December
- **Group Size:** Min. 1 Pax
- **Start/End:** Lukla (fly from Kathmandu)

The Gokyo Lakes are sacred to both Hindus and Buddhists and are counted among the Five Sacred Lakes of Nepal. The trek passes through Sagarmatha National Park, crosses the Ngozumpa Glacier — the largest glacier in the Khumbu — and follows the scenic Gokyo Valley, an off-the-beaten-path alternative to the classic Everest Base Camp route.

---

## Key Highlights

- Reach Gokyo Lakes (4,790m) — the world's highest freshwater lakes, sacred to Hindus and Buddhists
- Summit Gokyo Ri (5,360m) for panoramic views of Everest (8,848m), Cho Oyu (8,188m), Lhotse (8,516m), Makalu (8,485m), and the Ngozumpa Glacier
- Walk across Ngozumpa Glacier — the largest glacier in the Khumbu region
- Enter Sagarmatha National Park, a UNESCO World Heritage Site
- Visit Namche Bazaar — the Sherpa trading hub and cultural heart of the Khumbu
- Hike to Everest View Hotel (3,880m) — the world's highest luxury hotel, with 360-degree views of Cho Oyu, Thamserku, and surrounding peaks
- Experience warm Sherpa hospitality and traditional teahouse culture
- Spot diverse wildlife: Himalayan snowcocks, grazing yaks, and various bird species
- Thrilling scenic flight to Tenzing-Hillary Airport, Lukla

---

## Best Time to Trek

- **Spring (March–May):** Clear skies, stable weather, best mountain visibility
- **Autumn (September–November):** Dry trails, crisp air, most popular season

> **Note:** During peak seasons (March–May and October–November), Lukla flights operate from Manthali Airport (Ramechhap) due to congestion at Tribhuvan Airport — a 5-hour drive from Kathmandu.

Monsoon (June–August) brings heavy rain and leeches on lower trails. Winter (December–February) is cold but possible for experienced trekkers.

## Detailed Itinerary

- **Day 01:** Arrival in Kathmandu — airport transfer, trek briefing, rest
- **Day 02:** Free day in Kathmandu — preparation and rest
- **Day 03:** Fly to Lukla, trek to Phakding (2,652m) — 8 km, 3 hours along Dudh Koshi River
- **Day 04:** Trek Phakding to Namche Bazaar (3,440m) — 10–12 km, 6 hours; entry into Sagarmatha National Park
- **Day 05:** Acclimatization — hike to Everest View Hotel (3,880m) and return — 3–4 km, 3–4 hours; views of Everest, Cho Oyu, Thamserku
- **Day 06:** Trek Namche to Dole (4,200m) — 11 km, 5 hours; trail splits from EBC route into Gokyo Valley
- **Day 07:** Trek Dole to Machhermo (4,470m) — 6 km, 5 hours; altitude increases, views open up
- **Day 08:** Trek Machhermo to Gokyo Lake (4,790m) — 8–9 km, 4 hours; first view of the sacred Gokyo Lakes
- **Day 09:** Hike to Gokyo Ri (5,360m) and return — 5 km, 4 hours; sunrise views of Everest, Cho Oyu, Lhotse, Makalu, and Ngozumpa Glacier
- **Day 10:** Trek Gokyo to Dole (4,200m) — 12 km, 6 hours; begin descent
- **Day 11:** Trek Dole to Namche Bazaar (3,440m) — 11 km, 5 hours
- **Day 12:** Trek Namche to Lukla (2,860m) — 19 km, 7 hours
- **Day 13:** Fly Lukla to Kathmandu
- **Day 14:** Leisure day in Kathmandu — explore, shop, rest
- **Day 15:** Departure from Tribhuvan International Airport

## Difficulty & Fitness

Rated moderate. No technical climbing required. The trek suits fit beginners and seasoned trekkers alike.

Most demanding sections:
- **Namche to Dole (Day 6):** Significant altitude gain leaving the main EBC trail
- **Machhermo to Gokyo (Day 8):** High altitude approach, cold temperatures
- **Gokyo Ri summit (Day 9):** Steep climb at 4,790m+ — requires early start and slow pace
- **Namche to Lukla (Day 12):** Long descent day — 19 km, hard on knees

Key requirements:
- Proper acclimatization at Namche Bazaar (Day 5) is essential before ascending to Gokyo Valley
- Slow, steady pace above 4,000m
- Stay hydrated and monitor for altitude sickness symptoms

## The Gokyo Lakes

The Gokyo Lakes are a series of oligotrophic lakes located in the Gokyo Valley at altitudes between 4,700m and 5,000m. They are among the highest freshwater lakes in the world and are considered sacred by both Hindus and Buddhists — counted among the Five Sacred Lakes of Nepal.

The main lake at Gokyo village (4,790m) is the most visited. The valley contains six major lakes in total.

---

## Ngozumpa Glacier

The Ngozumpa Glacier is the largest glacier in the Khumbu region and one of the largest in the Himalayas. It flows from Cho Oyu (8,188m) and is clearly visible from Gokyo Ri. The trek passes directly alongside the glacier on the approach to Gokyo Lakes.

## Views from Gokyo Ri (5,360m)

From the summit of Gokyo Ri, on a clear day you can see:
- **Mount Everest** (8,848.86m) — world's highest peak
- **Cho Oyu** (8,188m) — 6th highest peak in the world
- **Lhotse** (8,516m) — 4th highest peak
- **Makalu** (8,485m) — 5th highest peak
- **Ngozumpa Glacier** — largest glacier in the Khumbu
- **Mahalangur Himal range** — full panoramic sweep

## Essential Packing List

- Daypack 25–30L with rain cover
- Sleeping bag rated to –20°C
- Down jacket (essential above 4,000m)
- 2L water bottles or hydration reservoir
- Headlamp with spare batteries (for early Gokyo Ri start)
- Trekking boots (waterproof, ankle support)
- Layered clothing: base layer, mid layer, waterproof outer shell
- Warm hat, buff, gloves
- Sunscreen SPF 50+, lip balm, sunglasses (UV400)
- Trekking poles (recommended for descents)
- Personal first aid and altitude sickness medication
