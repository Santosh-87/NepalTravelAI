# Annapurna Base Camp Trek

**Source:** https://www.nepalhikingteam.com/package/annapurna-base-camp-trek
**Last updated:** February 27, 2026

---

## Overview

The Annapurna Base Camp Trek is a classic 14-day Himalayan adventure through forests, traditional villages, and diverse landscapes, reaching 4,130 meters within the Annapurna Sanctuary.

- **Destination:** Nepal
- **Duration:** 14 Days
- **Difficulty:** Moderate
- **Max. Elevation:** 4,130m (Annapurna Base Camp)
- **Best Time:** March - May & September - December
- **Total Distance:** ~115 km (71 miles) on trail / ~295 km (183 miles) including transfers

The route enters the Annapurna Sanctuary — a natural amphitheater surrounded by Annapurna I (8,091m), Machhapuchhre, Annapurna South, Hiunchuli, and Gangapurna. The trek balances altitude acclimatization, mountain scenery, and cultural experiences, making it suitable for both beginners and seasoned trekkers.

The route also includes the classic via Ghorepani Poon Hill variation, adding panoramic sunrise views over the Annapurna and Dhaulagiri ranges before entering the sanctuary.

---

## Key Highlights

- Reach Annapurna Base Camp (4,130m) surrounded by a full 360-degree panorama of the Annapurna massif
- Witness panoramic sunrise from Poon Hill (3,210m) over Dhaulagiri, Annapurna, and Machhapuchhre
- Views of world-class peaks: Dhaulagiri I (7th highest), Annapurna I (10th highest), Machhapuchhre (Fishtail), and Hiunchuli
- Visit Machhapuchhre Base Camp (3,700m) — a quiet, culturally significant stop beneath Fishtail Peak
- Trek through rhododendron forests, bamboo valleys, river corridors, and alpine terrain
- Walk through traditional Gurung and Magar villages and observe mountain lifestyles
- Unwind at natural hot springs in Jhinu Danda after completing the high-altitude section
- Stay in family-run teahouses with warm meals along the entire route
- Stay in Pokhara, the city of lakes and one of Nepal's most popular tourist destinations

---

## Best Time to Trek

- **Spring (March–May):** Clear skies, rhododendrons in bloom, stable temperatures, reliable trail conditions
- **Autumn (September–November):** Best mountain visibility, dry trails, most popular season

Monsoon (June–August) brings heavy rain and slippery trails. Winter (December–February) is cold but possible for experienced trekkers with the right gear.

---

## Permits Required

- Annapurna Conservation Area Permit (ACAP) — checked at Birenthanti and Chhomrong checkpoints
- TIMS Card (Trekkers' Information Management System)

## Detailed Itinerary

- **Day 01:** Arrival in Kathmandu — airport transfer, pre-trek briefing, gear distribution
- **Day 02:** Kathmandu to Pokhara by tourist bus — 6 to 7 hours drive along Prithvi Highway
- **Day 03:** Drive to Hile trailhead, trek to Ulleri (1,960m) — 3.2 km, 2 hours trekking
- **Day 04:** Trek to Ghorepani (2,880m) — 7 km, 4–5 hours through rhododendron forests via Banthanti and Nangethanti
- **Day 05:** Early sunrise hike to Poon Hill (3,210m), then trek to Chuile (2,300m) — 11.5 km, 7–8 hours via Deurali Pass
- **Day 06:** Trek to Chhomrong (2,170m) — 5.8 km, 5 hours; steep descent to Kimrong Khola then climb to Chhomrong
- **Day 07:** Trek to Dovan (2,600m) — 8.5 km, 5–6 hours through bamboo and rhododendron forests via Lower and Upper Sinuwa
- **Day 08:** Trek to Deurali (3,230m) — 6.2 km, 4–5 hours via Himalaya and Hinku Cave
- **Day 09:** Trek to Annapurna Base Camp (4,130m) — 6.5 km, 5–6 hours via Machhapuchhre Base Camp (3,700m)
- **Day 10:** Descend from ABC to Bamboo (2,310m) — 13 km, 7–8 hours via Deurali, Himalaya, and Dovan
- **Day 11:** Trek to Jhinu Danda (1,780m) — 11 km, 6 hours via Chhomrong; afternoon soak in natural hot springs
- **Day 12:** Short hike to Samrung roadhead (~4.5 km, 2 hours), drive to Pokhara (2–3 hours)
- **Day 13:** Drive from Pokhara to Kathmandu — 204 km, 6–7 hours by tourist bus
- **Day 14:** Breakfast and departure from Tribhuvan International Airport

## Difficulty & Fitness

The ABC trek is graded moderate. No technical climbing is required. Daily walks range from 4 to 7 hours with steady altitude gain. The route suits first-time high-altitude trekkers with reasonable fitness.

Most demanding sections:
- Ulleri stone steps on Day 3 — steep sustained climb
- Descent and re-ascent between Chuile and Chhomrong (Day 6)
- Steep descent from Chhomrong to Chhomrong Khola then climb back up (Day 11)
- Final approach to ABC across glacial terrain at altitude (Day 9)
- Long descent from ABC to Bamboo — hard on knees (Day 10)

---

## Trail & Landscape

The trek passes through dramatically varied terrain:
- **Lower section (Hile to Ghorepani):** Terraced farmland, stone paths, dense rhododendron forest
- **Mid section (Ghorepani to Chhomrong):** Forested ridges, river crossings, Gurung village of Chhomrong
- **Upper section (Chhomrong to ABC):** Bamboo forest, narrowing glacial corridor, alpine moraine, open sanctuary basin

Important trail notes:
- Meat is not available or encouraged from Chhomrong onward due to remoteness and religious beliefs
- Loud music is prohibited beyond Chhomrong
- ACAP permit is checked at Birenthanti (Day 3) and Chhomrong (Day 6)
- The Hinku Cave section near Deurali is prone to avalanche paths in winter/spring and rockfall in monsoon
- Accommodation shifts from private bathrooms (lower trail) to shared bathrooms (upper trail) above Chhomrong

## Essential Packing List

- Daypack 25–30L with rain cover
- Sleeping bag rated to –20°C
- Down jacket rated to –15°C or lower
- 2L water bottles or hydration reservoir
- Headlamp with spare batteries
- Trekking boots (waterproof, ankle support)
- Layered clothing: base layer, mid layer, waterproof outer shell
- Sun hat, warm hat, buff, gloves
- Sunscreen SPF 50+, lip balm, sunglasses (UV400)
- Trekking poles (strongly recommended for steep descents)
- Personal first aid and medications
- Dry bags for electronics and documents
