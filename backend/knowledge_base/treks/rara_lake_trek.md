# Rara Lake Trek

**Source:** https://www.nepalhikingteam.com/package/rara-lake-trek
**Last updated:** February 27, 2026

---

## Overview

The Rara Lake Trek is a 17-day off-the-beaten-path journey to the largest and most pristine lake in Nepal, located in the remote far-western Rara National Park. This trek is ideal for nature lovers and solitude seekers who want to experience untouched wilderness far from the crowds of the popular Everest and Annapurna trails.

- **Destination:** Nepal (Far-Western Region)
- **Duration:** 17 Days
- **Difficulty:** Moderate
- **Max. Elevation:** 3,710m (Ghurchi Mara Ridge)
- **Rara Lake Elevation:** 3,062m
- **Best Time:** March–May & September–November
- **Access:** Fly Kathmandu → Nepalgunj → Jumla

Rara Lake sits at 3,062 meters, surrounded by coniferous forest and framed by snow-capped Himalayan peaks. Rara National Park was established in 1976 to protect this pristine ecosystem. The park preserves 1,074 species of flowers, 51 species of mammals, and 214 species of birds.

---

## Key Highlights

- Admire Rara Lake (3,062m) — Nepal's largest and most pristine freshwater lake, shimmering beneath snow-capped mountains
- Trek through Rara National Park — home to musk deer, leopards, red panda, and the national bird of Nepal (Danfe/Himalayan Monal)
- Explore the ancient village of Sinja — once the capital of the Malla kings, with historical stones along the trail
- Visit the 300-year-old Kanaksundari Temple on the route via Sinja
- Cross Ghurchi Mara Ridge (3,710m) for panoramic views of the lake and surrounding Himalayas
- Swim in Rara Lake — cold, crystal-clear, and refreshing
- Climb the wooden observation tower near the National Park Headquarters for views over the lake and hills
- Trek through birch, pine, walnut, and bamboo forests filled with medicinal plants
- Experience the remote far-western culture of Nepal, rarely visited by international trekkers
- Two rest days at Rara Lake to fully explore the area

---

## Best Time to Trek

- **Spring (March–May):** Stable weather, clear mountain views, forests in bloom
- **Autumn (September–November):** Dry trails, crisp air, best visibility

Monsoon (June–August) brings heavy rain and difficult trail conditions. Winter (December–February) is very cold at altitude and some trails may be snow-covered.

## Access & Route Logic

Unlike most Nepal treks, Rara requires two domestic flights:
1. **Kathmandu → Nepalgunj** (commercial flight, ~1 hour)
2. **Nepalgunj → Jumla** (30-minute scenic mountain flight)

The trek begins and ends in Jumla, with the return via the same flight route back through Nepalgunj to Kathmandu.

## Detailed Itinerary

- **Day 01:** Arrival in Kathmandu — airport transfer, briefing, overnight hotel
- **Day 02:** Kathmandu Valley sightseeing — Kathmandu Durbar Square, Swayambhunath, Boudhanath, Pashupatinath; trek preparation
- **Day 03:** Fly Kathmandu to Nepalgunj — explore the city; visit Bageshwori Temple (one of Nepal's most important Hindu temples); overnight hotel
- **Day 04:** Fly Nepalgunj to Jumla, trek to Chala Chaur (2,900m) — 30-minute scenic flight to Jumla; trek north through Jumla Valley, pass Buddhist stupas at Micha village (built ~15th century); overnight tented camp
- **Day 05:** Cross the pass (~3,500m) — ascend through birch and pine forests; views of Kanjiroba peak with its twin summits; descend to meadow campsite near shepherd huts
- **Day 06:** Trek via Sinja (2,440m) to Botan (2,600m) — walk along Jaljala Khola; pass 300-year-old Kanaksundari Temple; visit Sinja village (ancient capital of Malla kings); continue through gorge to Botan
- **Day 07:** Trek to Rara National Park border — follow trail along Ghatta Khola through beautiful valley; enter Rara Lake National Park; overnight at Gorosingha
- **Day 08:** Trek to Rara Lake (3,062m) — ascend to Ghurchi Mara Ridge (3,710m) for panoramic views; first view of Rara Lake to the northeast; descend through bamboo and birch forest, cross Khatyar Khola, arrive at the lake
- **Day 09:** Rest day at Rara Lake — explore the lakeshore, visit old Rara village, climb observation tower behind the post office for views, visit National Park Headquarters; swimming allowed in the lake
- **Day 10:** Rest/exploration day at Rara Lake — further explore the lake and surroundings at leisure
- **Day 11:** Trek via Jhyari (2,630m) to river camp — skirt the lake, cross ridge into Jhyari village, follow terraced farmland along Jhyari Khola toward Dhauligar Khola valley; overnight river camp
- **Day 12:** Cross Ghurchi Lagna Pass (3,450m) to Chautha (2,770m) — pass through Pina village, ascend to Ghurchi Lagna Pass with small chortens and alpine pastures; descend to Chautha
- **Day 13:** Trek to Tharamara (3,280m) — follow northern bank of Sinja Khola past Kabra; pass through villages of Bumra and Nyawre; note: Silajit (medicinal mineral) found in this area
- **Day 14:** Trek to Jumla — ascend via Khali Lagna and Danphe Lekh ridge for mountain views; descend steeply back to Jumla; explore Jumla market town
- **Day 15:** Fly Jumla to Nepalgunj, then Nepalgunj to Kathmandu; overnight hotel
- **Day 16:** Leisure day in Kathmandu — shopping, sightseeing, rest
- **Day 17:** Transfer to Tribhuvan International Airport — departure

## Difficulty & Fitness

Rated moderate. No technical climbing required. The main challenges are remoteness, altitude, and multi-day camping in a tent rather than teahouses.

Most demanding sections:
- **Ascent to Ghurchi Mara Ridge (3,710m) on Day 8** — steep but manageable
- **Ghurchi Lagna Pass crossing (3,450m) on Day 12** — steep descent into Chautha
- **Return to Jumla via Danphe Lekh (Day 14)** — steep descent requiring caution

Key notes:
- This is a **camping trek** — tented camps throughout, not teahouse lodges
- Remote area with limited rescue infrastructure — travel insurance with helicopter evacuation is essential
- Minimum group size: 15+ (check current requirements)

## Rara National Park

- Established: 1976
- Purpose: Protect Rara Lake from sedimentation and human encroachment
- **Flora:** 1,074 species of flowers, extensive medicinal plant cultivation
- **Mammals:** 51 species including musk deer, Himalayan black bear, ghoral, leopard, red panda
- **Birds:** 214 species including Danfe (Himalayan Monal — Nepal's national bird)
- **Fish:** Snow trout and other native species in the lake
- **Forest type:** Predominantly coniferous with birch, bamboo, and walnut zones

---

## Rara Lake Facts

- Nepal's largest lake
- Elevation: 3,062m
- Surrounded by Rara National Park on all sides
- Sacred and pristine — no motorised boats allowed
- Crystal-clear water — swimming is permitted
- Observation tower near the National Park Headquarters offers panoramic views of the lake and hills

---

## Nepalgunj — Transit Hub

Nepalgunj is the gateway city for the far-western region. Located in the Terai lowlands close to the Indian border:
- Hot and humid climate — very different from Kathmandu
- Bageshwori Temple — one of Nepal's most important Hindu temples
- Strong Indian cultural influence in food, language, and daily life

---

## Sinja Valley — Historical Significance

Sinja village was the ancient capital of the Malla kings of western Nepal. Historical stones and inscriptions line the trail through the village, offering insight into the Malla dynasty that ruled this region centuries ago.

- Personal first aid and altitude sickness medication
