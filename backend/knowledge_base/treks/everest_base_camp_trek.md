## Overview

The Everest Base Camp Trek is a classic Himalayan trek in Nepal that leads to the foot of Mount Everest through Sherpa villages, monasteries, and the Khumbu Valley.

- **Destination:** Nepal
- **Duration:** 16 Days
- **Difficulty:** Moderate to Strenuous
- **Max. Elevation:** 5,545m (Kala Patthar)
- **Best Time:** March - May & September - December
- **Total Distance:** 138 km (85.7 miles)

The trek begins with a scenic 30-minute flight to Lukla (2,860m), the gateway to the Everest region. From Lukla, the trail follows the Dudh Koshi Valley, passing through Sherpa villages including Namche Bazaar, Tengboche, and Dingboche while gradually ascending through the Khumbu region.

The trek culminates at Everest Base Camp at 5,364 meters, located at the edge of the Khumbu Icefall. The highest point is Kala Patthar at 5,545 meters, offering panoramic views of Everest and surrounding peaks. The highest sleeping point is Gorak Shep at 5,164 meters.

The 16-day itinerary includes 11 days of trekking with two acclimatization days at Namche Bazaar and Dingboche. On the return journey, the trail descends via Pheriche rather than Dingboche — a shorter and quieter route.

## Key Highlights

- Stand at Everest Base Camp (5,364m) at the foot of the world's highest mountain
- Climb Kala Patthar (5,545m) at sunrise for close-up views of Everest, Lhotse, Nuptse, Pumori, and Khumbu Glacier
- Acclimatization hike to Nangkartshang Hill (5,083m) in Dingboche with views of Makalu and Island Peak
- Visit the Everest View Hotel (3,880m), the world's highest luxury hotel
- Encounter four 8,000-meter peaks: Everest (8,848.86m), Lhotse (8,516m), Makalu (8,485m), Cho Oyu (8,188m)
- Visit Tengboche Monastery (Dawa Choling Gompa), the largest monastery in the Khumbu
- Explore Pangboche Monastery (oldest in the region) and Khumjung Monastery (famed for its Yeti skull relic)
- Trek through Sagarmatha National Park, a UNESCO World Heritage Site
- Walk through Thukla Pass, where memorials honour climbers Rob Hall, Scott Fischer, and Babu Chiri Sherpa
- Acclimatise in Namche Bazaar (3,440m), the cultural and commercial heart of the Khumbu

## Best Time to Trek

- **Spring (March–May):** Stable weather, dry trails, comfortable daytime temperatures, reliable Lukla flights
- **Autumn (September–November):** Clear skies, best mountain views, most popular season

Monsoon (June–August) brings heavy rain and leeches on lower trails. Winter (December–February) is cold but possible for experienced trekkers.

## Difficulty & Fitness

The EBC trek is rated moderate to strenuous. It is a non-technical journey — no climbing or ropes required. It is suitable for fit hikers with good endurance, even without prior high-altitude experience. The key challenge is altitude, not technical terrain.

Most demanding sections:
- Ascent to Namche Bazaar from Monjo — steep and long
- Acclimatization hike to Nangkartshang Hill (5,083m) — physically demanding
- Route from Gorak Shep to EBC — rocky moraine paths
- Ascent to Kala Patthar — sustained uphill climb at altitude

---

## Trail & Landscape

- Lower trail (Lukla to Tengboche): Pine, rhododendron, and fir forests
- Mid trail (Tengboche to Dingboche): Alpine terrain, first clear views of Everest
- Upper trail (Dingboche to EBC): Fully open alpine terrain above treeline, moraine paths alongside Khumbu Glacier
- Several sections include stone staircases, especially around Lukla
- Microspikes recommended for Kala Patthar during cold/snowy conditions
