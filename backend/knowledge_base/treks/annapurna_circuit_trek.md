# Annapurna Circuit Trek

**Source:** https://www.nepalhikingteam.com/package/annapurna-circuit-trek
**Last updated:** February 27, 2026

---

## Overview

The Annapurna Circuit Trek is a 14-day classic route circling the Annapurna Massif, passing through subtropical valleys, river gorges, high-altitude deserts, and glacial terrain. The trek begins in Jagat and concludes in Jomsom, crossing the highest point — Thorong La Pass at 5,416 meters.

- **Destination:** Nepal
- **Duration:** 14 Days
- **Difficulty:** Moderate to Strenuous
- **Max. Elevation:** 5,416m (Thorong La Pass)
- **Best Time:** March - May & September - December
- **Start Point:** Jagat (1,300m)
- **End Point:** Jomsom (2,720m), then fly to Pokhara

The trek showcases Nepal's most dramatic natural contrasts — from lush green forests of the Marshyangdi River Valley to the barren, Tibet-like Kali Gandaki Valley. Daily walking sessions average 5–6 hours over nine trekking days, allowing proper acclimatization.

---

## Key Highlights

- Cross Thorong La Pass (5,416m), one of the highest mountain passes in the world
- Visit Muktinath Temple — one of Nepal's holiest Hindu and Buddhist pilgrimage sites with 108 holy water spouts and an eternal flame
- Explore Kagbeni — one of the oldest villages in the Himalayas, with medieval mud houses and dark tunnels
- Views of Manaslu, Annapurna II, III, IV, Lamjung Himal, Dhaulagiri, Nilgiri, Chulu East and West
- Visit Nepal's largest apple orchard in Bhratang (Barthang) village — ~67,000 trees
- Acclimatization day in Manang Valley with optional hikes to Gangapurna Lake and Milarepa Cave
- Visit centuries-old Braka Sangag Samling Gompa monastery
- Experience Gurung, Thakali, and Magar cultural communities
- Scenic 20-minute mountain flight from Jomsom to Pokhara
- Trek through Annapurna Conservation Area — Nepal's largest protected area

---

## Best Time to Trek

- **Spring (March–May):** Stable weather, clear skies, rhododendrons in bloom
- **Autumn (September–November):** Best visibility, dry trails, most popular season

Monsoon (June–August) brings heavy rainfall and trail hazards. Winter (December–February) is extremely cold at high altitude — Thorong La Pass may be snow-blocked.

---

## Permits Required

- Annapurna Conservation Area Permit (ACAP)
- TIMS Card (Trekkers' Information Management System)

Permits are checked at Tal village (Day 3) and Ledar (Day 9).

## Detailed Itinerary

- **Day 01:** Arrival in Kathmandu — airport transfer, trek briefing, gear distribution, rest
- **Day 02:** Drive Kathmandu to Jagat (1,300m) — 186 km, 8–9 hours along Marshyangdi River via Besisahar, Bhulbule, Ngadi, Syange
- **Day 03:** Trek Jagat to Dharapani (1,860m) — 12–14 km, 6 hours via Tal, Chamche, and Karte; permit check at Tal
- **Day 04:** Trek Dharapani to Chame (2,670m) — 15–16 km, 6 hours via Bagarchhap, Danakyu, Timang, Koto; views of Manaslu and Annapurna II
- **Day 05:** Trek Chame to Pisang (3,300m) — 14–15 km, 5 hours via Barthang (apple orchard), Dhukure Pokhari; views of Paungda Danda and Annapurna ranges
- **Day 06:** Trek Pisang to Manang (3,540m) — 16 km, 5–6 hours via Upper Pisang, Gyaru, Ngawal, Braka; visit Braka Sangag Samling Gompa
- **Day 07:** Acclimatization day in Manang — optional hike to Gangapurna Lake or Milarepa Cave (2–3 km, 2–3 hours)
- **Day 08:** Trek Manang to Yak Kharka (4,050m) — 11 km, 3–4 hours via Tengi, Gunsang; vegetation thins, yak pastures begin
- **Day 09:** Trek Yak Kharka to Thorong Phedi (4,450m) — 8 km, 3–4 hours via Ledar (permit check), Deurali; optional continue to High Camp
- **Day 10:** Cross Thorong La Pass (5,416m), trek to Muktinath — 16.5 km, 7–8 hours; depart 4:30am, reach pass after ~5–6 hours, descend via Thorung Khola and Chambar Bhir
- **Day 11:** Trek Muktinath to Jomsom (2,720m) — 16–17 km, 5–6 hours via Jharkot, Kagbeni, Ekle Bhatti; morning visit to Muktinath Temple
- **Day 12:** Fly Jomsom to Pokhara (825m) — 20-minute mountain flight; rest at Pokhara lakeside
- **Day 13:** Drive Pokhara to Kathmandu — 204 km, 7–8 hours by tourist bus
- **Day 14:** Departure from Tribhuvan International Airport

## Difficulty & Fitness

The circuit is rated moderate to strenuous. No technical climbing required but Thorong La Pass crossing is the most demanding section.

Most challenging sections:
- Thorong La Pass crossing (Day 10) — 4:30am start, sustained 5–6 hour ascent, thin air, rocky terrain, possible snow
- Pisang to Manang (Day 6) — longest day with significant elevation gain
- Narrow landslide-prone trail between Ledar and Thorong Phedi (Day 9)

Important notes:
- Walk slowly and stay hydrated above 3,500m
- The acclimatization day in Manang (Day 7) is non-negotiable for safety
- Thorong La Pass crossing should begin at 4:30am to avoid afternoon winds

## Trail & Landscape

The circuit passes through four dramatically different zones:

| Zone | Section | Landscape |
|---|---|---|
| Subtropical | Jagat to Dharapani | River gorges, waterfalls, suspension bridges |
| Temperate forest | Dharapani to Pisang | Pine and rhododendron forests, waterfalls |
| Alpine | Pisang to Manang | Open valleys, yak pastures, Tibetan-style villages |
| High altitude desert | Manang to Muktinath | Barren rocky terrain, glacial moraines, prayer flags |
| Kali Gandaki Valley | Muktinath to Jomsom | Deep river gorge, wind-swept valley, Thakali culture |

## Thorong La Pass — Critical Information

- **Elevation:** 5,416m — one of the highest trekable mountain passes in the world
- **Start time:** 4:30am from Thorong Phedi or High Camp — mandatory to avoid afternoon winds
- **Total crossing distance:** ~16.5 km (Thorong Phedi to Muktinath)
- **Duration:** 7–8 hours total
- **Conditions:** Rocky terrain, possible snow, false summits, switchbacks
- **Decorated with:** Prayer flags, shrines, spiritual items at the summit
- **Views from pass:** Annapurna massif, Great Barrier Ridge separating Manang from the Mustang region

---

## Muktinath Temple

- One of the holiest pilgrimage sites in Nepal for both Hindus and Buddhists
- Dedicated to Hindu god Vishnu / recognized as Chenrizeg (Avalokiteshvara) by Buddhists
- Features 108 holy water spouts and an eternal flame
- Devotees practice spiritual cleansing by bathing in the icy water from the spouts

## Essential Packing List

- Daypack 25–30L with rain cover
- Sleeping bag rated to –20°C
- Down jacket (mandatory for Thorong La Pass and above 4,000m)
- 2L water bottles or hydration reservoir
- Headlamp with spare batteries (essential for 4:30am pass start)
- Trekking boots (waterproof, ankle support)
- Layered clothing: base layer, mid layer, waterproof outer shell
- Warm hat, buff, gloves (critical for pass crossing)
- Sunscreen SPF 50+, lip balm, sunglasses (UV400)
- Trekking poles (strongly recommended for pass descent)
- Personal first aid and altitude sickness medication

---

## Flight Note — Jomsom to Pokhara

The 20-minute Jomsom–Pokhara mountain flight is frequently delayed or cancelled due to strong winds in the Kali Gandaki Valley, especially in the afternoon. If cancelled, the overland return via Marpha, Ghasa, Tatopani, and Beni to Pokhara takes approximately 5 hours by jeep.
