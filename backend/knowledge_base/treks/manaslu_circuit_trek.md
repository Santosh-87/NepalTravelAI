# Manaslu Circuit Trek

**Source:** https://www.nepalhikingteam.com/package/manaslu-circuit-trek
**Last updated:** February 27, 2026

---

## Overview

The Manaslu Circuit Trek is a 14-day off-the-beaten-path trek circling Mount Manaslu (8,163m) — the world's 8th highest peak — within Nepal's restricted Manaslu Conservation Area. The route was opened to trekkers in 1991 and remains largely untouched by modern development, preserving natural beauty and cultural integrity through limited trekker numbers.

- **Destination:** Nepal
- **Duration:** 14 Days
- **Difficulty:** Moderate to Strenuous
- **Max. Elevation:** 5,106m (Larkya La Pass)
- **Best Time:** March–May and September–November
- **Total Distance:** ~125 km (77 miles)
- **Trekking Days:** 10 days of walking
- **Minimum Group Size:** 2 persons (restricted area requirement)
- **Start Point:** Machha Khola (900m)
- **End Point:** Tilije, then drive to Kathmandu

This is a restricted area trek. Limited trekker numbers keep trails quiet and authentic, with traditional Tibetan-influenced villages, ancient monasteries, colorful prayer flags, and sacred mani walls throughout.

---

## Key Highlights

- Cross Larkya La Pass (5,106m) — the highest and most dramatic point of the trek, with panoramic views of Manaslu, Himlung Himal, Cheo Himal, and the Annapurna ranges
- View Mount Manaslu (8,163m) — the world's 8th highest peak — up close from Samagaun village
- Visit Manaslu Base Camp (4,800m) or Birendra Lake (Birendra Taal) on acclimatization day
- Trek through Manaslu Conservation Area — rhododendron, pine, oak, and bamboo forests filled with medicinal herbs
- Follow the rushing Budi Gandaki River through quiet forest paths and suspension bridges
- Spot Himalayan tahr, snow leopards, red pandas, Himalayan monal, and golden eagles
- Explore Tibetan-influenced villages of Samagaun and Samdo with ancient monasteries
- Visit Pung Gyen Gompa and catch glimpses of the Punggen Glacier near Lho Gaun
- Descend through the Marsyangdi Valley, joining the famous Annapurna Circuit trail near Dharapani

---

## Best Time to Trek

- **Spring (March–May):** Stable weather, clear mountain views, rhododendrons in bloom
- **Autumn (September–November):** Best visibility, dry trails, ideal conditions for Larkya La Pass crossing

Monsoon (June–August) brings heavy rainfall, leeches, and trail hazards. Winter (December–February) — Larkya La Pass may be snow-blocked and extremely cold.

---

## Permits Required

The Manaslu Circuit is in a **restricted area** — special permits are required and a minimum of 2 trekkers is mandatory.

- Manaslu Restricted Area Permit (RAP)
- Manaslu Conservation Area Permit (MCAP)
- Annapurna Conservation Area Permit (ACAP) — for the final section joining the Annapurna Circuit

## Detailed Itinerary

- **Day 01:** Arrival in Kathmandu — airport transfer, trek briefing, gear distribution, rest
- **Day 02:** Drive Kathmandu to Machha Khola (900m) — 160 km, 9 hours; start of trek region
- **Day 03:** Trek Machha Khola to Jagat (1,340m) — 22 km, 7–8 hours along Budi Gandaki River
- **Day 04:** Trek Jagat to Deng (1,860m) — 20 km, 8–9 hours through forested river valley
- **Day 05:** Trek Deng to Namrung (2,630m) — 19.5 km, 7–8 hours; mountain views begin to open
- **Day 06:** Trek Namrung to Shyala (3,575m) — 10.5 km, 4–5 hours; glimpses of Punggen Glacier and Pung Gyen Gompa
- **Day 07:** Trek Shyala to Samagaun (3,520m) — 8–9 km, 4–5 hours; close views of Mount Manaslu
- **Day 08:** Acclimatization day at Samagaun — optional hike to Manaslu Base Camp (4,800m) via Birendra Taal (Birendra Lake)
- **Day 09:** Trek Samagaun to Samdo (3,875m) — 8 km, 4–5 hours; Tibetan-influenced village
- **Day 10:** Trek Samdo to Dharmasala (4,460m) — 11.5 km, 4–5 hours; final camp before pass
- **Day 11:** Cross Larkya La Pass (5,106m), trek to Bhimtang (3,720m) — 24.5 km, 8–9 hours; earliest start required
- **Day 12:** Trek Bhimtang to Goa (2,515m) — 19 km, 6–7 hours; pine forest returns, altitude drops
- **Day 13:** Trek to Tilije (1.5 km), drive to Kathmandu — 215 km, 8–9 hours
- **Day 14:** Departure from Tribhuvan International Airport

---

## Difficulty & Fitness

Rated moderate to strenuous. No technical climbing required but Larkya La Pass is demanding.

Most challenging sections:
- **Larkya La Pass crossing (Day 11):** Longest and hardest day — 24.5 km, 8–9 hours, high altitude, early start required, possible snow and ice
- **Deng to Namrung (Day 5):** Long day with significant elevation gain
- **Samdo to Dharmasala (Day 10):** High altitude approach to final camp before pass

Key requirements:
- Minimum 2 trekkers (restricted area regulation)
- Good physical fitness and prior trekking experience recommended
- Acclimatization day at Samagaun (Day 8) is non-negotiable

## Trail & Landscape

| Section | Terrain |
|---|---|
| Machha Khola to Deng | River valley, suspension bridges, gorges along Budi Gandaki |
| Deng to Namrung | Dense forests of rhododendron, pine, oak, and bamboo |
| Namrung to Samagaun | High alpine terrain, glacier views, Tibetan-style villages |
| Samagaun to Larkya La | Glacial moraines, snowfields, high-altitude desert |
| Larkya La to Bhimtang | Dramatic descent, open alpine meadows |
| Bhimtang to Tilije | Pine forest return, terraced hillsides, Marsyangdi Valley |

## Wildlife

The Manaslu Conservation Area is home to:
- Himalayan tahr
- Snow leopard
- Red panda
- Himalayan monal (national bird of Nepal)
- Golden eagle
- Diverse medicinal herbs and alpine flora

## Essential Packing List

- Daypack 25–30L with rain cover
- Sleeping bag rated to –20°C (essential — teahouses above 3,500m are unheated)
- Down jacket (mandatory for Larkya La Pass and above 4,000m)
- 2L water bottles or hydration reservoir
- Headlamp with spare batteries (essential for early pass start)
- Trekking boots (waterproof, ankle support, crampon-compatible recommended)
- Layered clothing: base layer, mid layer, waterproof outer shell
- Warm hat, buff, gloves, and spare gloves
- Sunscreen SPF 50+, lip balm, sunglasses (UV400)
- Trekking poles (strongly recommended for pass descent)
- Gaiters (useful in snow near the pass)
- Personal first aid and altitude sickness medication
