# Ghorepani Poon Hill Trek

**Source:** https://www.nepalhikingteam.com/package/ghorepani-poon-hill-trekking
**Last updated:** February 27, 2026

---

## Overview

The Ghorepani Poon Hill Trek is a short 8-day journey into the Annapurna region — one of Nepal's most popular and accessible treks. It is ideal for beginners, families, and anyone with limited time who wants to experience spectacular Himalayan scenery, Gurung and Magar village culture, and a sunrise from one of Nepal's most iconic viewpoints.

- **Destination:** Nepal (Annapurna Region)
- **Duration:** 8 Days
- **Difficulty:** Moderate (suitable for beginners)
- **Max. Elevation:** 3,210m (Poon Hill)
- **Best Time:** January–May & September–December
- **Group Size:** Min. 1 Pax
- **Start:** Kathmandu → Pokhara → Birethanti (drive)
- **End:** Ghandruk → Pokhara → Kathmandu

The trek is suitable for all ages and fitness levels. The trail passes through dense rhododendron and oak forests, traditional Gurung and Magar villages, terraced farmland, and stunning Himalayan viewpoints — all within 8 days.

---

## Key Highlights

- Sunrise from Poon Hill (3,210m) — panoramic views of Dhaulagiri I, Annapurna I, Annapurna South, Nilgiri, Machhapuchhre, and Hiunchuli
- Trek through dense rhododendron forests — spectacular in spring bloom (March–April)
- Walk through traditional Gurung and Magar villages including Ulleri and Ghandruk
- Visit the Gurung Museum in Ghandruk — a living showcase of Gurung ethnic history and culture
- Climb the famous 3,300 stone steps from Tikhedhunga to Ulleri
- Evening boating on Fewa Lake in Pokhara with reflections of Machhapuchhre and the Annapurna range
- Experience authentic Nepali cuisine — dal bhat, local teahouse meals
- Walk through alpine meadows full of wildflowers where mountain goats graze

---

## Best Time to Trek

- **Spring (March–May):** Rhododendrons in full bloom, clear skies, warm temperatures — most popular season
- **Autumn (September–November):** Best mountain visibility after monsoon, dry and stable trails
- **Winter (December–February):** Cold but possible; trail may have snow above Ghorepani; fewer crowds
- **Summer (June–August):** Monsoon season — trails are lush but wet and slippery

The trek can be done year-round, each season offering a different experience.

## Detailed Itinerary

- **Day 01:** Arrival in Kathmandu — airport transfer, briefing, gear distribution, rest
- **Day 02:** Drive Kathmandu to Pokhara — 204 km, 6–7 hours along Prithvi Highway; evening walk at Fewa Lake
- **Day 03:** Drive Pokhara to Birethanti, trek to Ulleri (1,960m) — 9–10 km, 5 hours; ascend 3,300 stone steps from Tikhedhunga via north bank of Bhurungdi Khola
- **Day 04:** Trek Ulleri to Ghorepani (2,880m) — 9–10 km, 5 hours; climb through pastures and forest via Nangethanti; views of Dhaulagiri I (8,167m), Nilgiri (6,940m), Annapurna I (8,091m), Hiunchuli (6,441m)
- **Day 05:** Early morning hike to Poon Hill (3,210m) for sunrise, return to Ghorepani for breakfast, trek to Tadapani (2,630m) — 9 km, 7 hours total
- **Day 06:** Trek Tadapani to Ghandruk — 5–6 km, 4 hours through forest; visit Gurung Museum; drive to Pokhara (55 km, ~2 hours)
- **Day 07:** Drive Pokhara to Kathmandu — 204 km, 6–7 hours; receive trek completion certificate on arrival
- **Day 08:** Departure — transfer to Tribhuvan International Airport

## Difficulty & Fitness

Rated moderate and suitable for beginners, families, seniors, and first-time trekkers. No technical terrain or high-altitude risks.

Most demanding sections:
- **Tikhedhunga to Ulleri (Day 3):** ~3,300 steep stone steps — tiring but manageable
- **Ulleri to Ghorepani (Day 4):** Steady uphill through forest — slow and steady pace recommended
- **Poon Hill sunrise hike (Day 5):** Early start (before dawn), cold morning temperatures at 3,210m

## Poon Hill Sunrise

Poon Hill (3,210m) is one of the most iconic viewpoints in Nepal. The sunrise view is the main attraction of the entire trek.

**Mountains visible from Poon Hill on a clear morning:**
- Dhaulagiri I (8,167m) — 7th highest mountain in the world
- Annapurna I (8,091m) — 10th highest mountain in the world
- Annapurna South (7,219m)
- Nilgiri (6,940m)
- Machhapuchhre / Fishtail Peak (6,993m)
- Hiunchuli (6,441m)
- Tukuche Peak, Myagdi Matha, and more

The hike from Ghorepani to Poon Hill takes 45–60 minutes. Depart before dawn for the best sunrise experience. Mornings are cold — a down jacket is essential.

## Ghandruk Village

Ghandruk is one of the largest Gurung villages in Nepal, situated at approximately 1,940m with commanding views of Annapurna South, Hiunchuli, and Machhapuchhre.

- Home to the **Gurung Museum** — showcases traditional Gurung culture, costumes, weapons, and history
- Traditional stone-roofed houses set on steep terraced hillsides
- One of the best-preserved ethnic villages in the Annapurna region
- Starting point for several alternative treks toward Annapurna Base Camp

## Cultural Communities on the Route

- **Magar people:** Ulleri village — one of Nepal's indigenous hill communities
- **Gurung people:** Ghandruk village — known for their role as Gurkha soldiers and mountain guides
- **Tamang people:** Villages along the lower trail

## Essential Packing List

- Daypack 20–25L with rain cover
- Sleeping bag rated to –10°C (teahouses provide blankets but nights are cold)
- Down jacket (essential for Poon Hill sunrise at 3,210m)
- 2L water bottles or hydration reservoir
- Headlamp with spare batteries (for pre-dawn Poon Hill hike)
- Trekking boots (waterproof, ankle support)
- Layered clothing: base layer, mid layer, light waterproof shell
- Warm hat, buff, gloves (cold mornings above 2,500m)
- Sunscreen SPF 50+, lip balm, sunglasses
- Trekking poles (strongly recommended for 3,300 stone steps descent)
- Light rain jacket (year-round)
- Personal first aid kit
