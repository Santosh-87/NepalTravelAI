## Nagarkot

Nagarkot is a tiny mountain village located on the outskirts of Kathmandu. The village lies on top of a hill that commands incredible views of the Himalayas. Surrounded by greenery and traditional farms, Nagarkot is an ideal place to chill after a long trek. This place also offers a welcome respite from the chaos and pollution of Kathmandu.

Tourists love Nagarkot for its mesmerizing sunrises and sunsets. It's the only place close to Kathmandu from where you can view Mount Everest and other Himalayan mountains without having to trek or hike for multiple days. Nagarkot is also a popular honeymoon destination. Its pristine nature, breathtaking mountain views, and spellbinding sunrises and sunsets create the perfect mood for romance.

## Nagarkot Elevation  Height and Altitude of Nagarkot Above Sea Level

Nagarkot, located in the Bhaktapur District of Nepal, sits at an elevation of approximately 2,195 meters (7,200 feet) above sea level. This altitude provides visitors with panoramic views of the Himalayas, including glimpses of Mount Everest on clear days.

It's important to note that elevation readings for Nagarkot can vary slightly across different sources, ranging from 1,905 meters (6,250 feet) to 2,200 meters (7,218 feet). These discrepancies are due to the undulating terrain of the region.

## Temperature and Climate

Nagarkot, situated at an altitude of 2,175 meters, generally experiences a cooler climate than Kathmandu, with daytime temperatures ranging from 20C to 25C and night time temperatures significantly lower. It's cooler during the day and chilly at night, especially in the evenings.

Winter (DecemberFebruary): Nagarkot experiences chilly winters, with average daytime temperatures ranging from 5C to 15C. Nighttime temperatures can drop below freezing, occasionally reaching as low as -5C. Snowfall is rare but possible, adding a serene charm to the landscape.

Spring (MarchMay): Spring brings mild and pleasant weather, with daytime temperatures between 15C and 25C. Nights remain cool in Nagarkot, averaging 5C to 10C. The season is marked by blooming rhododendrons and clear skies, offering excellent mountain views.

Summer/Monsoon (JuneAugust): Summers are warm and humid in Nagarkot, with daytime highs ranging from 20C to 30C. Night temperatures hover around 15C to 20C. The monsoon season brings frequent rainfall, contributing to the lush greenery but also causing occasional travel disruptions.

Autumn (SeptemberNovember): Autumn is considered the best time to visit Nagarkot, featuring clear skies and comfortable temperatures between 15C and 25C during the day. Nights are cooler, ranging from 5C to 15C. The season offers unobstructed views of the Himalayas, making it ideal for trekking and photography.

## Location  Where is Nagarkot located?

Nagarkot is a renowned hill station situated in the Changunarayan Municipality of Bhaktapur District, within Bagmati Province, Nepal. Positioned approximately 32 kilometers east of Kathmandu, it offers a tranquil retreat from the bustling capital. From Bhaktapur, the distance is 14 kilometers. It's one of the few places close to Kathmandu where one can experience snowfall during the winter season.

## History of Nagarkot

In the earlier days, Nagarkot served as a strategic fort (kot is fort in Nepali) for the rulers of Kathmandu Valley. From here the army could guard and watch out for enemy incursions into their territory. During the 19th and 20th centuries, Nagarkot became a popular summer retreat among the Shah royals and the Rana nobles.

Nagarkot, situated on a ridge overlooking the Kathmandu Valley, has a rich history rooted in its strategic location and the beautiful views it offers. Originally, it served as a fort for the rulers of Kathmandu Valley, guarding against potential invasions. Later, it became a popular summer retreat for the Shah royals and Rana nobles, and later still, a hill station for British colonial residents. Finally, Nagarkot transitioned into a popular tourist destination in the mid-20th century, known for its stunning views of the Himalayan mountains, including Mount Everest.
- Ancient Fort: Nagarkot's strategic location on a hilltop allowed it to serve as a watchtower and defense point for the Kathmandu Valley. The name "kot" itself translates to "fort" in Nepali.
- Royal Retreat: During the 19th and 20th centuries, Nagarkot became a favored escape for the Nepalese royal families and Rana nobles seeking respite from the summer heat and the bustling city life.
- British Hill Station: The cool climate and panoramic views of the Himalayas attracted British officers and colonial residents during the British colonial period in India.
- Modern Tourist Hub: The breathtaking views of the Himalayas, including Mount Everest, began to draw tourists from around the world in the mid-20th century. Its proximity to Kathmandu, a 32-kilometer drive away, further facilitated its growth as a popular tourist destination.

## How to get to Nagarkot from Kathmandu?

A winding asphalt road connects Nagarkot to Kathmandu and Bhaktapur. It takes about an hour and a half to drive from Kathmandu to Nagarkot. From Bhaktapur, it takes a little over thirty minutes to reach the hilly town.

You can also hike to Nagarkot from Changu Narayan, Chisapani Sundarijal, and Sankhu. The hiking trails traverse dense forests, small hills, paddy fields, pretty Newari and Tamang villages, and local farms.

There's also a helipad in Nagarkot used by helicopter companies to operate helicopter tours.

---

## Kathmandu to Nagarkot Distance

Private Vehicle: Opting for a private car or taxi is the most convenient and time-efficient method. The journey typically takes around 1.5 hours, depending on traffic and road conditions. Hiring a private vehicle provides flexibility, comfort, and the opportunity to enjoy the picturesque route at your own pace.

Public Bus: For a more economical option, public buses are available. The trip involves taking a bus from Kathmandu to Bhaktapur, followed by a transfer to another bus heading to Nagarkot. The entire journey can take approximately 3 to 4 hours. While this mode is budget-friendly, it may be less comfortable and more time-consuming.

Hiking: For the adventurous, hiking from nearby villages like Sankhu to Nagarkot is an option. This trek offers an immersive experience through local communities and natural settings, taking about 5 hours to complete.

## Transportation

If you are on a tight budget, you can use local buses. It's difficult to find direct buses to Nagarkot from Kathmandu. You can board a local bus up to Kamal Binayak in Bhaktapur and catch another bus heading to Nagarkot from the bus stop.

If money is not an issue and you want to travel comfortably, you can hire a private taxi to drive you to Nagarkot. In case you don't want to stay overnight in Nagarkot, you can book a taxi for a full day and head to Nagarkot, explore the village, and return to Kathmandu the same day.

You can also rent a motorbike or a mountain bike and ride to Nagarkot. Cycling up the narrow and curvy road to Nagarkot offers a thrilling experience.

## Mountains Visible from Nagarkot

From Nagarkot, you can see the vast Himalayan mountain range. On a clear day, you can see the horizon covered by the tall summits of some of the highest mountains in the world. Nagarkot offers splendid views of Langtang, Annapurna South, Annapurna I and II, Mount Everest, Jugal Himal, Manaslu, Ganesh Himal, Gaurishankar, Dorje Lakpa, Shisapangma, Huinchuli, Gyaltzen Peak and the Mahalangur range. The hill station is known for offering panoramic views of these majestic peaks, making it a popular destination for sunrise viewing and hiking.

## What to pack?

Apart from the essential items, do not forget to carry warm clothes. As Nagarkot lies at a much higher altitude than Kathmandu, it gets chilly, especially at night. Most hotels don't have central heating and the rooms get pretty cold as dusk falls. Dressing up warmly while enjoying the sunrise will protect you from the icy cold wind.

## Best Time to Visit Nagarkot

You can visit Nagarkot any time of the year. But to enjoy crystal clear views of the Himalayas, you need to time your travel during the drier autumn or winter months. There's snowfall during mid-winter, which turns this small mountain village into a winter wonderland.

Located above 2000 meters, Nagarkot gets its fair share of cloudy and misty weather. During the summer season, it's usually shrouded in thick fog and mist. While you can't see the mountains, the wispy and cottony clouds covering the forests and hills lend a different charm and beauty to the place.

Check the weather forecast: Before your trip, check the weather forecast to ensure you have the best chance of clear views.

Consider your interests: If you're interested in trekking, spring and autumn are the best times. If you enjoy winter activities, consider visiting in December or January, but be prepared for colder temperatures and potential snowfall.

Book accommodations in advance: Nagarkot can become popular during peak season, so booking accommodations in advance is recommended.

Nagarkot is a place that deeply immerses you in nature. This hilly village will let you enjoy awesome views of the Himalayan mountains without having to take a long trek into the mountains. Nagarkot offers the perfect refuge to unwind and relax if you are seeking an escape from the chaos of busy cities.
