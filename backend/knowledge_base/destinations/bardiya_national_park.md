## Bardiya National Park

Are you looking for a tour package to explore the wildlife of Nepal? If you do, check out the Bardia National Park! This wildlife tour package has safari tours, bird-watching activities, visits to museums and offers a high chance of having a glance at the endangered tiger.

On the journey, well be entering the Bardia National Park, which covers almost 374 sq mi in the Terai region of Nepal. Before, it was known as the Royal Bardia Wildlife Reserve and was set aside as a royal hunting reserve in the 1960s. Then, in 1988 the government finally proclaimed the area as a protected region and named it Bardia National Park.

A long time ago, people had rural settlements around the valley, but then later, they were shifted and resettled somewhere else. Now, the area is completely protected.

In a nutshell, this tour package is captivating as it will provide you with an opportunity to enjoy the wildlife and also learn about the cultures and rich heritages of the Tharu community.

## Route Overview

Here is a quick route overview for this wildlife tour package.

The tour begins with a morning flight from Kathmandu to Nepalgunj, which takes approximately 50 minutes. Upon landing at the airport, well take a drive to Bardia National Park. After reaching the hotel, you can relax at the hotel and visit the nearby villages.

The next morning, a jeep safari tour is organized. As we start the journey, you might get the chance to watch different mammals like wild tigers, swamp deer, and also rhinos. Later, well get off the jeep and then head towards the Tharu museum. The Tharu Museum contains several artifacts like jewellery, kitchen utensils, old pots, and many more. Then, well return to the hotel and stay there for the night.

In the morning, after savoring delicious breakfast, well carry on with the plans for that day. Bird watching activity is arranged where youll get to watch varied species of birds and enjoy the sights. Then, well continue heading into the jungle to watch the other animals in their natural habitats. Well return to the hotel and prepare for the departure.

An afternoon flight will be scheduled from Nepalgunj to Kathmandu, and well soar high in the sky, bidding farewell to a lovely trip.

## When is the best time for the Bardia National Park?

The Wildlife tour is all about watching the animals and immersing yourself in the jungle. Hence, the best time when you can see these animals are from January- May and September- December. These spring, autumn, and winter season are perfect for these tours.

We recommend you skip the monsoon season as the trails can get muddy, and the animals will not be visible outside. Hence, we suggest you choose the best time.
