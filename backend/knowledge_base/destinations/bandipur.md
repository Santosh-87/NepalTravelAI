## Overview

Bandipur is a beautifully preserved hilltop Newar town in central Nepal, located between Kathmandu and Pokhara. It is one of Nepal's best-kept secrets — a car-free, 18th-century town where time seems to stand still, with stunning Himalayan views, centuries-old architecture, and authentic hill culture untouched by mass tourism.

- **Location:** Tanahun District, Gandaki Province, Central Nepal
- **Elevation:** ~1,030m
- **Distance from Pokhara:** 76.5 km (~2 hours by bus)
- **Distance from Kathmandu:** ~145 km (~5–6 hours)
- **Nearest Airport:** Pokhara International Airport
- **Recommended Stay:** 2–3 nights

Bandipur was historically an important stop along the India–Tibet trade route. The village has retained its Newari architectural character — brown-shuttered windows, carved wooden balconies, white, orange, and red plaster walls — and motorised vehicles are not permitted inside the village, keeping it quiet and pedestrian-friendly.

## Key Highlights

- **Car-free main bazaar** — completely pedestrian, lined with 18th-century Newari architecture
- **Siddha Gufa (Siddha Cave)** — Nepal's largest cave and Asia's second largest, 500 meters deep; 1.5-hour walk from Bandipur
- **Thani Mai Temple** — hilltop temple 30 minutes from the centre with 360-degree views of Bandipur town, the Annapurna range, and surrounding valleys; best at sunrise and sunset
- **Bandipur Tundikhel** — open ground with panoramic Himalayan views
- **Hiking trails** through tribal villages, verdant forests, and hilltop shrines
- **Ramkot village hike** — 5 km from Bandipur (2–2.5 hours walk), traditional clay-and-wood village with Himalayan views and rice terraces
- **Silk factory** — see traditional silk production (seasonal — best outside November)
- **Authentic Newari culture** — temples, shrines, sacred caves, traditional festivals, and local cuisine

## Things To Do

### 1. Stroll the Old City Centre
Walk along the car-free Bazaar (main street) and side streets to see centuries-old Newari houses, local life, and valley views. Try sweet deep-fried dough from local stalls and coffee at a teahouse.

### 2. Hike to Thani Mai Temple
- 30-minute walk (130m climb with stone stairs) from the town centre
- Small Hindu temple at the summit
- **360-degree panoramic view** of Bandipur, the Annapurna range, and surrounding hills
- Best visited at **sunrise or sunset** — considered the best viewpoint Bandipur has to offer

### 3. Visit Siddha Gufa (Siddha Cave)
- Nepal's largest cave — 500 meters deep
- Asia's second largest cave
- 1.5-hour walk from Bandipur (mostly with valley views)
- Entry: ~Rs 200 for a 45-minute guided tour
- Note: Inside is slippery even with walking shoes; the hike back is uphill

### 4. Hike to Ramkot Village
- 5 km from Bandipur — 2 to 2.5 hours walk
- Traditional Gurung/Magar village with clay-and-wood houses
- Corn drying on rooftops, goats and chickens roaming freely
- Views of green rice fields, valleys, and Himalayan peaks
- Carry your own food and water — no restaurants in the village
- Mountain View Hotel at the end of the village (check if open)

### 5. Visit the Silk Factory
- Observe silk worm cultivation and garment processing
- Seasonal — active periods are spring and early summer (closed in late autumn/winter)

| Restaurant | Type | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| The Himalayan Cafe | Cafe | Good coffee, excellent momos, cozy wooden interior |
| Bandipur Chhen | Breakfast | Thick pancakes, yogurt with fruit and muesli; proceeds support local children's education |
| Falcha | Local Nepali | Popular with locals; nut salads, Chatamari (rice-flour pizza with egg); low prices |
| Samay Baji | Newari cuisine | Traditional Newari dishes — lentil pancakes, dried rice, potato salad; next to the temple at the end of the bazaar |
| The Old Inn | Upscale | Terrace dining overlooking Himalayas; Nepali and Newari menu (~Rs 890 per set); pricier |
| Pratiksha Restaurant | Snacks | Cheap local snacks, samosas, lassi, simple breakfast |

**Must try local dishes:**
- Samay Baji — the classic Newari mixed platter (dried rice, lentil pancakes, potato salad, dried meat)
- Chatamari — Newari rice-flour crepe with egg, sometimes called "Newari pizza"
- Momos — steamed or fried dumplings
- Dal Bhat — traditional Nepali rice and lentil meal

## Getting To Bandipur

### From Pokhara (~3 hours)
1. Take a minivan or tourist bus toward Kathmandu/Dumre — get off at **Dumre** (~Rs 300–600)
2. From Dumre take a taxi (~Rs 500) or local bus (~Rs 30 pp, every 45 minutes) for the last 8 km to Bandipur
- Total time: ~3 hours from Pokhara

### From Kathmandu (~5–7 hours)
1. Take a tourist bus to Pokhara — ask driver to drop you at **Dumre** (2 hours before Pokhara)
- Ticket: Rs 700–2,500 (negotiate for Dumre drop)
2. From Dumre take a taxi (~Rs 500) or local bus (~Rs 30 pp) to Bandipur
- Total time: ~5–7 hours from Kathmandu

### By Air
- Nearest airport: **Pokhara International Airport** (~76.5 km, ~2 hours by bus)

## Practical Tips

- **No motorised vehicles** are allowed inside Bandipur village — it is entirely pedestrian
- Recommended stay: **2–3 nights** to cover all main attractions comfortably
- The village is **less touristy** than Pokhara or Kathmandu but does receive Nepali domestic tourists
- Bandipur is a convenient **stopover** between Kathmandu and Pokhara
- Carry cash — ATMs are limited; the nearest reliable ATM is in Dumre
- Best Himalayan views are in the **early morning** before clouds build up
